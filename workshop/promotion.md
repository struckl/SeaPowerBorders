**Title**

Sea Power Borders – the borders, capitals and naval bases of your mission's date on the tactical map

**Body**

The stock map only shows coastlines. This mod adds a **Borders** and a **Cities and bases** layer that follow the date of the mission:

- **Land borders of the day**: the Soviet Union whole until 1991, the inner-German border and West Berlin until October 1990, Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia, North and South Yemen, the Vietnam DMZ, Hong Kong and Macau by their years. Disputed borders and lines of control (Korea, Kashmir, the Golan, Cyprus) are dashed
- **Capitals, cities and naval bases of the day**: East Berlin and Bonn, Leningrad, Karl-Marx-Stadt; anchors in blue for NATO and its allies, red for the Warsaw Pact; bases only in the years they were used (Holy Loch until 1992, Cam Ranh Bay Soviet 1979–2002)
- An **optional coastal distance line** (3, 6 or 12 nm off the coast), off by default
- Switch in the map menu → Map Layers, or Shift+F7 (borders) and F7 (cities)

**Heads-up – please read:** the mod only draws on the map; it changes nothing in the game or in any mission. But the lines are a **reference map, not the rules of your mission**. Missions set their own areas and rules, and they often differ from real geography – *Northern Vigil* announces "weapons free" from its own 10 nm circle, not from the coast or a border; *Don't Mess With Texas* fails you for attacking an airbase. In missions that say "don't escalate", follow the briefing and the mission's own areas (Map Layers → On Map Areas), never a line from this mod. The coastal distance line is one distance for every country, **not** a territorial limit (in 1985 Norway claimed 4 nm, the US and UK 3, Greece and Turkey 6 in the Aegean, the Soviet Union 12). Borders are today's lines with date rules – land only, approximate, no sea borders or airspace.

**Data**: borders and extra cities from Natural Earth (public domain); cities, ports and airfields from the game's own lists, read from your install; date rules, the naval-base list and the Berlin Wall / Vietnam DMZ lines are my own and approximate; city ranking uses today's populations.
